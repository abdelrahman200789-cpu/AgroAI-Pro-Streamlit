TITLE: Soil pH — basic interpretation

Soil pH affects nutrient availability and microbial activity. Suitable pH depends on crop and soil conditions; one pH target does not fit all crops. A reliable soil test is preferable to guessing from plant appearance. Follow the soil-testing laboratory's sampling instructions and use local agronomic recommendations before applying lime, sulfur, or fertilizer.

This knowledge note is general education and is not a substitute for a soil test or local agronomist advice.
