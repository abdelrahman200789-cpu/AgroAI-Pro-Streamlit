TITLE: Crop planning — factors to check

Crop selection depends on season, local climate, soil texture and drainage, soil test results, water availability, variety, planting date, market access, and local pest pressure. A simple table match can narrow options but cannot replace local recommendations. Verify crop suitability for the specific district and planting window before making a commercial decision.

The crop records in AgroAI Pro are demonstration data. They are not an official planting calendar and should be expanded with local agricultural research or extension data before field use.
