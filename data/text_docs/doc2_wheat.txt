TITLE: Powdery mildew — wheat scouting

Powdery mildew is a fungal disease of cereals including wheat. It can produce white, powdery patches on leaves and stems; severity and appearance depend on the crop stage and environment. Yellowing can occur but is not specific to this disease. Do not diagnose from one symptom alone.

Integrated management notes: inspect several plants and both sides of leaves; consider cultivar resistance and local disease forecasts; avoid unnecessary nitrogen applications that can encourage dense growth; and consult local extension guidance for thresholds and treatment decisions. Use only plant-protection products registered for wheat in your location and follow the product label.
